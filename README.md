# 📸 Instagram Performance Analytics for Sugar Cosmetics
### 50,000 posts · Apr 2024 – Apr 2025 · Tableau Dashboard

🔗 **[View the Live Interactive Dashboard on Tableau Public →](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/hariharan.reddy.r/viz/InstagramPerformanceAnalyticsforSugarCosmetics_17899874184030/Instagram)**

## 📌 Problem Statement
Analyze Sugar Cosmetics' Instagram performance and design an interactive Tableau
dashboard to recommend strategic social media decisions — covering engagement,
reach, content-type performance, hashtags, and posting patterns.

**Scope:** 50,000 posts · 12 months (Apr 2024 – Apr 2025) · Image/Video/Reel/Story formats

## 🔧 Tools Used
Tableau · Tableau Public · Calculated Fields & KPIs · Filters & Dashboard Actions
· Visual Storytelling

## 🧭 Approach
10 Tableau tasks combined into one interactive dashboard:
1. KPI Overview
2. Monthly Posting Trend
3. Top 10 Posts by Likes
4. Top 10 Posts by Engagement Rate
5. Post Type Performance Comparison
6. Day of Week Analysis
7. Engagement Funnel
8. Hashtag Analysis
9. Final Combined Dashboard
10. Strategic Recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Consistency is the account's core strength** — steady 4.1K–4.3K posts/month for
  12 straight months, driving a healthy 18.55% engagement rate across 987M reach
- **No single format dominates** — Top 10 posts by likes differ by under 3%; Video
  appears most often in the Top 10 by engagement rate (4 of 10), all clustered
  33.8–34.1%
- **Timing, hashtags, and format barely move the needle** — posting day (18.42–18.63%),
  hashtags (18.52–18.67%), and format (2,941–2,964 avg. likes) all sit within a ~1% band
- **The funnel is healthy but Shares & Saves lag** — 77% of 1.28B impressions convert
  to reach, but Shares (8.1M) and Saves (8.9M) are the smallest engagement actions
- Higher posting *volume* did not translate to higher *engagement* — quality is the driver

## ✅ Strategic Recommendations
- Maintain the current posting cadence — don't chase volume, protect the 18.55% baseline
- Redirect effort from scheduling/hashtag optimization toward content quality —
  none of those levers move engagement meaningfully
- Keep a balanced format mix, leaning slightly into Video for engagement consistency
- Build save-worthy, shareable hooks into every content brief (how-to/educational
  content, "save this" / "share with a friend" CTAs) to fix the funnel's weakest stage
- Use 18.55% engagement rate as the go-forward campaign benchmark

## 🎯 Business Impact
Gave the marketing team a clear, evidence-based priority: the account is consistent
everywhere except where it counts — content quality and the Shares/Saves stage of
the funnel — rather than timing, hashtags, or format, which is where effort was
previously assumed to matter most.
